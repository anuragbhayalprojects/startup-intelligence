# Startup Action Framework

## Purpose

This framework helps AI determine the most appropriate action after evaluating a startup.

The objective is to move from information to action.

---

# Action Categories

## Ignore

Conditions:

* No clear ICICI use case
* Weak traction
* Low strategic relevance

Action:

No further action required.

---

## Monitor

Conditions:

* Emerging startup
* Early-stage technology
* Future relevance possible

Action:

Track developments and reassess periodically.

---

## Founder Meeting

Conditions:

* Strategic fit identified
* Additional information required

Action:

Schedule introductory discussion.

---

## Internal Discussion

Conditions:

* Potential business relevance identified

Action:

Share internally for feedback.

---

## Demo Day

Conditions:

* Multiple business teams may benefit
* Strong innovation potential

Action:

Include in startup showcase sessions.

---

## Business Introduction

Conditions:

* Clear use case identified
* Relevant team identified

Action:

Connect startup with business stakeholders.

---

## Technical Evaluation

Conditions:

* Business interest confirmed

Action:

Assess security, architecture, integration feasibility, compliance.

---

## Proof of Concept (POC)

Conditions:

* Technical feasibility confirmed
* Business sponsor available

Action:

Pilot implementation.

---

## Commercial Engagement

Conditions:

* POC successful

Action:

Commercial deployment.

---

## Strategic Investment Review

Conditions:

* High strategic relevance
* Strong growth potential
* Significant future value

Action:

Investment evaluation.

---

# Decision Matrix

| Strategic Fit | Deployability | Recommended Action          |
| ------------- | ------------- | --------------------------- |
| Low           | Low           | Ignore                      |
| Medium        | Low           | Monitor                     |
| Medium        | Medium        | Founder Meeting             |
| High          | Medium        | Business Introduction       |
| High          | High          | POC                         |
| Very High     | High          | Strategic Investment Review |
