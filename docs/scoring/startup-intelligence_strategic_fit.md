# Startup Intelligence OS – Strategic Fit Framework

## Purpose

The Strategic Fit Framework determines how valuable a startup could be for the ICICI Group after initial relevance has already been established.

Strategic Fit is different from Relevance.

* Relevance answers: "Is this startup relevant to any ICICI Group entity?"
* Strategic Fit answers: "How strategically valuable is this startup for ICICI Group?"

A startup can be highly relevant but have poor strategic fit due to weak execution, lack of traction, low deployability, or limited scalability.

The Strategic Fit Score is used by the Strategic Fit Agent to prioritize startups for business introductions, proof-of-concepts (POCs), commercial engagements, and strategic investment reviews.

---

# Strategic Fit Evaluation Dimensions

Total Score: 100

---

## 1. Business Problem Relevance

Weight: 25%

### Objective

Evaluate how effectively the startup solves an identified business problem within ICICI Bank or its group companies.

### Evaluation Criteria

High Score:

* Directly addresses a known ICICI business problem
* Clear and measurable value proposition
* Demonstrated outcomes with existing customers

Medium Score:

* Potentially relevant use cases
* Requires adaptation or customization

Low Score:

* Weak connection to identified business problems
* No obvious value creation

### Example

Startup:

AI-powered underwriting platform

Business Problem:

Manual underwriting and risk assessment

Result:

High score

---

## 2. Entity Alignment

Weight: 15%

### Objective

Evaluate how many ICICI entities could benefit from the startup.

### Evaluation Criteria

High Score:

* Relevant to multiple entities
* Clear cross-group applicability

Medium Score:

* Relevant to a single entity

Low Score:

* Limited applicability

### Example

Fraud Detection Platform

Relevant Entities:

* ICICI Bank
* ICICI Lombard
* ICICI Securities
* ICICI Prudential Life

Result:

High score

---

## 3. Business Team Alignment

Weight: 10%

### Objective

Evaluate whether a clear internal sponsor can be identified.

### Evaluation Criteria

High Score:

* Specific business team identified
* Clear ownership

Medium Score:

* Multiple possible teams

Low Score:

* No obvious business sponsor

### Example

Claims Automation Startup

Business Team:

Claims Operations

Result:

High score

---

## 4. Deployability

Weight: 15%

### Objective

Evaluate the feasibility of deploying the solution within ICICI environments.

### Evaluation Criteria

High Score:

* Enterprise-ready
* Secure architecture
* API-based integration
* Regulatory compliant

Medium Score:

* Requires moderate effort

Low Score:

* Significant implementation challenges

### Indicators

* Existing enterprise deployments
* Security certifications
* Cloud readiness
* API maturity

---

## 5. Market Validation

Weight: 10%

### Objective

Evaluate evidence of product-market fit.

### Evaluation Criteria

High Score:

* Paying customers
* Strong retention
* Revenue growth

Medium Score:

* Early traction

Low Score:

* Concept stage

### Indicators

* Customer count
* Revenue
* Customer references
* Growth metrics

---

## 6. Innovation & Differentiation

Weight: 5%

### Objective

Assess uniqueness and defensibility.

### Evaluation Criteria

High Score:

* Proprietary technology
* Significant differentiation

Medium Score:

* Some differentiation

Low Score:

* Commodity offering

### Indicators

* Patents
* Proprietary datasets
* Technical advantages

---

## 7. Scalability

Weight: 5%

### Objective

Assess ability to scale across business units and geographies.

### Evaluation Criteria

High Score:

* Scalable SaaS model
* Multi-entity applicability

Medium Score:

* Some scalability constraints

Low Score:

* Services-heavy model

---

## 8. Strategic Investment Potential

Weight: 5%

### Objective

Evaluate whether the startup could become a strategic investment candidate.

### Evaluation Criteria

High Score:

* Strong growth trajectory
* Strategic importance
* Long-term partnership potential

Medium Score:

* Potential future candidate

Low Score:

* Limited investment relevance

---

## 9. Ecosystem Influence

Weight: 5%

### Objective

Evaluate the startup's position within its ecosystem.

### Evaluation Criteria

High Score:

* Industry leader
* Strong partnerships
* Significant market visibility

Medium Score:

* Growing ecosystem presence

Low Score:

* Limited ecosystem recognition

---

# Strategic Fit Score Calculation

Strategic Fit Score =

Business Problem Relevance × 25%

*

Entity Alignment × 15%

*

Business Team Alignment × 10%

*

Deployability × 15%

*

Market Validation × 10%

*

Innovation & Differentiation × 5%

*

Scalability × 5%

*

Strategic Investment Potential × 5%

*

Ecosystem Influence × 5%

# Guiding Principles

1. Strategic relevance is more important than funding raised.

2. Business value is more important than publicity.

3. Customer adoption is more important than awards.

4. Deployability is more important than technical sophistication.

5. Long-term strategic value is more important than short-term trends.

6. Strategic Fit should never be evaluated without first establishing Relevance.

7. Strategic Fit scores should be explainable and supported by evidence.

8. Human review remains mandatory before major engagement or investment decisions.
