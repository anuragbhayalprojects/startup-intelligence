# ICICI Business Problems Framework

## Purpose

This document defines the key business problems faced by ICICI Group entities.

The AI should map every startup to one or more business problems before determining strategic relevance.

The preferred evaluation flow is:

Startup
→ Capability
→ Business Problem Solved
→ ICICI Entity
→ Business Team
→ Recommended Action

---

## Retail Banking

### Customer Acquisition

Problems:

* Rising acquisition costs
* Low conversion rates
* Digital onboarding friction
* Limited customer intelligence
* Customer churn

Desired Outcomes:

* Lower acquisition cost

* Better targeting
* Higher conversion
