# Overview

ICICI Home Finance focuses on providing housing finance and related lending solutions.

For startup intelligence purposes, ICICI HFC should be viewed as a mortgage lending, property financing, customer acquisition, risk assessment, and property ecosystem organization.

Key priorities include:

* Home loan growth
* Customer acquisition
* Credit quality
* Faster underwriting
* Property intelligence
* Collections efficiency
* Operational excellence
* Digital transformation
