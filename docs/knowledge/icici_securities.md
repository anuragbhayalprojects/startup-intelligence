# Overview

ICICI Securities is one of India's leading retail and institutional securities businesses.

Its primary businesses include:

* Retail Broking
* Wealth Management
* Investment Advisory
* Equity Trading
* Derivatives Trading
* Mutual Fund Distribution
* Portfolio Advisory
* Institutional Equities
* Research Services

For startup intelligence purposes, ICICI Securities should be viewed as an investor acquisition, investor engagement, wealth creation, advisory, and capital markets platform.

The organization seeks to:

* Increase investor acquisition
* Improve investor engagement
* Grow assets under advisory
* Improve customer retention
* Enhance advisory capabilities
* Improve trading experience
* Increase digital adoption
* Strengthen research and intelligence
