# Overview

ICICI Prudential AMC manages mutual funds and investment products for retail, affluent, institutional, and corporate investors.

For startup intelligence purposes, ICICI Prudential AMC should be viewed as an asset gathering, investor engagement, distribution, and investment management organization.

Key priorities include:

* Asset growth
* Investor acquisition
* Investor retention
* Distributor productivity
* Digital distribution
* Financial awareness
* Investor engagement
