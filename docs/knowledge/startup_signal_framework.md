# Startup Signal Framework

## Purpose

Signals help identify startups that are gaining momentum.

Signals should supplement strategic relevance, not replace it.

Strategic relevance always has higher importance than publicity.

---

# Positive Signals

## Enterprise Customer Wins

Weight: 10

Examples:

* Major BFSI customer acquired
* Fortune 500 deployment

Interpretation:

Strong validation.

---

## Revenue Growth

Weight: 10

Interpretation:

Strong market validation.

---

## Product Adoption

Weight: 9

Interpretation:

Customers find value.

---

## Strategic Partnerships

Weight: 9

Interpretation:

Distribution expansion.

---

## Geographic Expansion

Weight: 8

Interpretation:

Scaling capability.

---

## Leadership Hiring

Weight: 7

Interpretation:

Growth preparation.

---

## Funding Round

Weight: 6

Interpretation:

Investor confidence.

Do not treat as proof of success.

---

## Accelerator Participation

Weight: 5

Interpretation:

Early validation.

---

## Patent Activity

Weight: 5

Interpretation:

Technology differentiation.

---

## Awards and Recognition

Weight: 3

Interpretation:

Weak signal unless supported by traction.

---

# Negative Signals

## Leadership Exits

Weight: -5

---

## Layoffs

Weight: -6

---

## Regulatory Issues

Weight: -9

---

## Product Failure

Weight: -8

---

## Major Customer Loss

Weight: -8

---

## Data Breach

Weight: -10

---

# Signal Score Calculation

Signal Score =

## Sum(Positive Signals)

Sum(Negative Signals)

Scale:

0-20 = Weak Momentum

21-40 = Moderate Momentum

41-60 = Strong Momentum

60+ = Exceptional Momentum

Signal Score should never override strategic relevance.
