# Classification Rules

Every startup must have:

- Industry
- Sector
- Subsector

Every startup may have:

- Multiple Industry Relevance values
- Multiple Business Models
- Multiple Tags

Example:

Industry:
Artificial Intelligence

Sector:
Agentic AI

Subsector:
Customer Service Agents

Industry Relevance:
- BFSI
- Enterprise

Business Models:
- B2B
- SaaS

Tags:
- Customer Support
- Workflow Automation
- Voice AI

---

# Validation Rules

1. Industry must exist in taxonomy.
2. Sector must belong to selected Industry.
3. Subsector must belong to selected Sector.
4. Industry Relevance must be selected from approved values.
5. Business Models must be selected from approved values.
6. AI-generated classifications outside taxonomy are invalid.
