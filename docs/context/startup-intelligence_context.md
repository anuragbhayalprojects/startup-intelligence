# Startup Intelligence Operating System – Business Context

## Purpose
The Startup Intelligence Operating System exists to support the Startup Engagement & Investments function responsible for identifying, evaluating, tracking, engaging, and recommending startups relevant to the ICICI Group.

The platform is not a startup directory, CRM, news aggregation platform, or venture capital database.

The platform exists to help ICICI identify startups that can create measurable strategic value.

---

## ICICI Group Entities Covered
The platform currently focuses on six entities:
* ICICI Bank
* ICICI Lombard
* ICICI Securities
* ICICI Home Finance
* ICICI Prudential Life
* ICICI Prudential AMC

All startup evaluations, recommendations, rankings, and intelligence outputs must consider relevance to one or more of these entities.

---

## Core Mission
The primary question the platform must answer is:
"Which startups are most relevant to the ICICI Group, why are they relevant, who should engage them, and what action should be taken?"

The platform should identify startups capable of generating:

* Revenue opportunities
* Customer acquisition opportunities
* Product innovation
* Technology transformation

* Risk reduction
* Digital transformation
* Strategic partnerships
* Strategic Investment opportunities

---

## Desired Output
Every startup recommendation should clearly explain:
1. What the startup does.
2. Which business problem it solves.
3. Which ICICI entity can benefit.
4. Why the startup is strategically relevant.
5. Risks and limitations.
6. Recommended next action.

The platform should always move from information to action.
